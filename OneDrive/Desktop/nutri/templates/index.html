If You're in Crisis Right Now
  • 1 Remove yourself from immediate danger if possible
  • 2 Call a helpline — trained counselors are ready to listen
  • 3 Tell someone you trust what you are feeling
  • 4 Breathe slowly — inhale 4 counts, hold 4, exhale 4
  • 5 Go to emergency services if you are in immediate danger
